# Stakeholders

## Stakeholder register

| Stakeholder | What they care about | What they need from this project | Interest | Influence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales advisors | Having stock to sell, customers not leaving empty-handed | Simple low-stock alerts, alerts when gifts are running out | High | Low |
| Counter manager | Sales targets, accurate stock | A suggested order list before each order | High | High |
| Brand representatives | Promotion results, brand sales, claims handling | Discount vs GWP comparison, claims records | High | High |
| Inventory management | Not tying up cash in stock | Slow-moving product list, stock turnover | Medium | High |
| Loss prevention / store operations | Shrinkage rate, count differences | Shrinkage reasons, high-risk products | Medium | Medium |

## Interest and influence

| | Low influence | High influence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **High interest** | **Keep informed:** Sales advisors | **Manage closely:** Counter manager, Brand representatives |
| **Lower interest** | **Monitor:** Loss prevention | **Keep satisfied:** Inventory management |

## Engagement approach

| Stakeholder | How to engage |
| --- | --- |
| Counter manager | Review the suggested order list and reorder rules together. Final sign-off on the to-be process. |
| Brand representatives | Share the promotion analysis before each major event. Agree on how gift quantities are communicated. |
| Inventory management | Share slow-mover and stock cover reports monthly. |
| Sales advisors | Short briefing on low-stock and gift alerts. Collect feedback on what alerts are useful. |
| Loss prevention | Monthly shrinkage summary. Agree on how testers and damage are recorded. |

## Note

This is a personal portfolio project. Stakeholder needs are defined from frontline experience at a fragrance counter, not from formal interviews.
